Straight answers

Questions practice owners ask us.

Does it sound like a robot?+

No. Callers hear a natural, friendly voice and have a real back-and-forth. It introduces itself as an AI assistant up front, then simply takes care of the caller: answers their questions, books the appointment, confirms the details.

Can it handle customers who don't speak English?+

Yes. It's multilingual. It answers in English, Spanish, and other languages, so a language barrier doesn't cost you the job.

What if it can't answer something?+

It takes a detailed message or routes the call to you, and texts you a full summary either way. Nothing gets buried in a voicemail box.

Do I have to change my phone number?+

No. We forward your existing number, so your customers call the same number they always have. Nothing changes on your trucks or your signs.

What does it cost?+

Every shop is different, so we price it to your call volume and setup right in the demo: no surprises, no long contracts. Most practices find it costs less than the patients they're losing each month.

How long until it's live?+

Days, not months. We do the building, you approve it and go. Founding partners get moved to the front of the line.

What about patient privacy?+

We configure call handling around your privacy requirements, and the assistant never gives medical advice: it books, routes, and takes messages. Bring your compliance questions to the demo and we'll walk through exactly how your setup would work.
